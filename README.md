### Hey, I'm Lec

I'm studying Data Science and Mathematics at the University of Calgary and I enjoy building things. If I am not studying most of my free time goes into machine learning projects and personal applications that make learning math easier, usually whatever idea I can't stop thinking about that week.

**What I've been building**

- **[Oil Tank Detection](https://github.com/lecfranzff/Oil-Tank-Detection)**: Can a model trained on public aerial imagery find oil storage tanks somewhere it has never seen? I built a detector to answer that and tested it on a real crude oil terminal in Hardisty, Alberta. It found every floating-roof tank there and ignored the tanks it wasn't meant to flag. The next step is estimating how full each tank is from space.
- **[Used Car Price Predictor](https://github.com/lecfranzff/used_car_app)**: estimates used car prices from 358K Canadian listings. You can [try the live app](https://usedcarapp.streamlit.app/), and it'll even show you where it tends to get things wrong.
- **Healthcare Fraud Detection** (in progress): a classification project on public healthcare claims. The goal is to flag which claims are most likely fraudulent and figure out what actually sets them apart, so an investigator knows where to look first.
